TripJam · Collab · Journey analysis

Where the group-trip journey breaks

Six personas walked through invite → plan → travel. What v1 covers well, and the nine gaps worth building for — ranked by how many trips hit them and how hard they bite.

2026-08-11Grounded in collab-tier2 code + docs/collaboration specs

The personas

Every shared trip has an organizer; the rest of the cast varies. The journey has to work for all of them — including the two who never open the app enthusiastically.

P1
The Organizer
Creates the trip, invites everyone, does 80% of the planning, usually pays first. Today's primary persona — best served.
P2
The Co-planner
Opinionated friend who joins, votes, argues in chat, saves a style. Served well once inside; getting them inside is the friction.
P3
The Passenger
Comes along, contributes nothing unprompted. Wants to glance at "where are we staying" — not to plan. Barely served.
P4
The Non-user invitee
Has no TripJam account. Meets the product for the first time via an invite. Their first 90 seconds decide whether the group plans here or on WhatsApp.
P5
The Late joiner
Joins after routes were picked or the itinerary is built. Needs a catch-up and a lever, not a blank chat.
P6
The Treasurer
Tracks who paid what and who owes whom. On group trips this job always exists — currently it lives in Splitwise.

The gaps, ranked

P0 = blocks adoption of collab itself · P1 = blunts the value once adopted · P2 = horizon.

P0 · GAP 1Inviting a non-user by email is a dead endP4, P1
The invite field says "username or email", but invite_user_by_handle errors with user_not_found when the email has no account — and no email is ever sent. The organizer's most natural gesture (type a friend's email) fails exactly for the people the feature exists to recruit. The link flow covers it, but only if the organizer understands the distinction.
Build: on user_not_found for a valid email, send an invite email carrying the join link (one Resend template — a thin slice of Phase 4, not the whole notification stack). Until then: change the inline error to "No TripJam account for that email — send them the invite link instead" with a copy button.
Evidence: members.js invite_user_by_handle contract; v1-scope "email/push explicitly out of scope".
P0 · GAP 3No re-engagement loop — silence after the first visitevery persona
Everything that happens on a shared trip (invite, poll, vote, rework, IG finished, pool low) notifies only via the in-app bell. A group trip is inherently multi-session and multi-person: the moment a co-traveller closes the tab, the trip cannot reach them again. This is the deferred Phase 4, but the journey analysis says it's not a fast-follow — it's the circulatory system of every other collab feature. Polls without pings are polls nobody answers.
Build: Phase 4 minimal slice, in this order: (1) transactional email for invite + "poll opened" + "itinerary ready" (Resend, 3 templates); (2) push via the existing Capacitor shell for Android; (3) the daily digest last. notification_prefs is already drafted in migrations.
Evidence: implementation-plan Phase 4 (deferred); bell/while-you-were-away are in-app only.
P1 · GAP 6The trip's biggest decision has no group momentP1, P2, P5
Route selection — the single highest-stakes call — is unilateral: one member taps Select and builds, votes are advisory, and the new rework flow (correctly warned) replaces routes and votes wholesale. Meanwhile polls exist for days and activities but deliberately not for routes (Phase 7 deferred). The journey inverts the spec's priority: groups need the decision checkpoint at the route level more than at the activity level. Post-IG, a late joiner's style has no lever at all except chat.
Build: a lightweight consensus checkpoint, not full Phase 7: on shared trips, "Build my itinerary" from a route that others up-voted differently (or with open route votes) shows "2 of 3 haven't weighed in — ask the group / build anyway". One sheet, no new tables (votes exist). Full routes-as-poll + post-IG rebuild stays v1.1.
Evidence: route select path; Phase 7 deferral rationale "high effort × rare use" — rare only because votes are currently consequence-free.
P1 · GAP 9Expenses have no payer, no split, no settle-upP6, everyone after the trip
The expenses widget tracks amounts against the trip, but rows carry no paid_by and no split. "Who owes whom" is the one job every group trip generates without exception — and it's the job that keeps the group returning to the app after the trip ends (the moment TripJam currently loses them). The members infrastructure this needs is now fully built.
Build: add paid_by (member) + even-split-by-default to trip_expenses, a per-member balance line in the widget, and a "settle up" summary. Custom splits and payment links later. Rides entirely on existing members + realtime rails.
Evidence: BoardView expenses schema — no payer/split fields.
P1 · GAP 5The Passenger has no one-tap laneP3
Every contribution surface asks for typing: a free-text style, a chat message, a poll with options. The least-engaged member of every group — statistically the most common persona — will do none of it, and their silence then reads as consent. A reactions table already exists in the prod schema with no UI on top of it.
Build: (1) quick-tag pills on the style sheet (the spec's original 🏖🥾🍜 tags — cut from v1) so a style is three taps; (2) 👍/😍/😬 reactions on route cards and day cards wired to the existing table. Reactions feed Trippy as weak preference signals.
Evidence: prod table list includes reactions; PreferencesSheet is free-text only "per product decision".
P1 · GAP 4Joining is an airlock with no briefingP4, P5
The /join flow itself is solid (preview → accept, token survives sign-up). But post-accept, a newcomer lands in the full trip UI cold: no explanation of Trippy, routes vs magazine vs board, or what the group has already decided. "While you were away" can't help — it diffs against their last visit, which doesn't exist. First-time non-users (P4) get product onboarding and trip orientation compressed into the same bewildering moment.
Build: a one-time post-accept Trippy briefing in the shared chat, visible only to the joiner: "Welcome — here's the trip so far: 8 days in Ziro, route picked, hotels open. Two things you can do right now: share your style, vote on the open poll." Generated from existing trip state; one prompt, one card.
Evidence: JoinTrip.jsx accept path drops straight into the trip route.
P1 · GAP 2Share and Invite are two doors that look alikeP1
The header offers Share (public read-only /share/:token) and Invite (membership /join/:token) as sibling affordances. Organizers will hand would-be editors the read-only link — the failure is silent, and the co-traveller concludes "I can see it but can't do anything", which reads as a broken product rather than the wrong link.
Build: one share sheet, two roles: "Invite to plan together — they join the trip" and "Share a view-only link". Same two tokens underneath; purely a chrome merge.
Evidence: Share button + Invite/avatar-stack coexist in the trip header.
P1 · GAP 7Pool money moments are individually awkwardP6, P2
When the shared pool runs dry, the fork paywall lands on whoever happened to press the button — a co-traveller who may feel it isn't their place to fund the group's AI budget. There's no "ask the group to chip in", no "low pool" signal to anyone else (compounded by Gap 3), and no way for the organizer to pre-commit "I'll cover this trip".
Build: a "Request top-up" action on the fork paywall that posts a card into the shared chat (chat is the one place everyone already is), plus an owner setting "charge my wallet when the pool is empty".
Evidence: fork-paywall path (emptyTripPool) targets only the triggering user.
P2 · GAP 8Day-of mode & post-trip close-outall, during/after travel
During the trip the app still presents its planning face; the group's needs shrink to "what's next, where, how do we get there, what did we spend". Afterwards there's no recap, no settle-up ceremony, no photo-anchored memory — the natural viral artifact ("look at the trip we planned on TripJam") goes unmade. Story mode is the obvious foundation for both.
Build later: a "travelling now" state (today-centric view, offline-first, expense quick-add) and a post-trip recap built on story mode + the settle-up from Gap 9. Sequenced after P0/P1 gaps.
Evidence: no date-aware mode switch exists; offline covers cached days only.
P2 · found bugGhost styles: removed members keep steering the AIedge case, but wrong
Member removal deletes poll votes but keeps trip_preferences rows. The style pipelines filter rows only by non-empty text and resolve names via the members list — so a removed member's style still feeds RG/IG/chat, attributed to "Traveler". The group plans around a person who isn't coming.
Fix (small, do with next collab commit): filter style lists to current member ids in the three call sites (RG body, IG body, chat preferencesList) — or delete the prefs row in remove_member/leave_trip.
Evidence: stylesForLLM / preferencesList filter on prefs_text only; spec: removal deletes votes, "everything else stays".

What the journey already gets right

Worth naming, so the gap list reads as prioritization rather than criticism:

  • Invite mechanics — link + username hybrid, preview before join, token survives sign-up, revoke, trip-full and expired states.
  • Trust & transparency — activity log on everything, attributed chat, best-effort undo with conflict warnings, while-you-were-away.
  • Money safety — pooled credits with a protected personal wallet; nobody's card is ever surprise-charged for group AI.
  • Live-ness — realtime sync for routes, board, polls, presence of new members.
  • Style loop (this week) — owner auto-counted, save acknowledged, rebalance chip, rework nudge with per-traveller attribution.
  • Group-aware AI — Trippy names who wanted what and proposes compromises; the raw material now flows from every member.

Suggested build order: Gap 1's error-copy stopgap + the ghost-styles fix (days) → Phase 4 minimal slice, Gaps 1+3 together (the multiplier for everything else) → route consensus checkpoint (Gap 6) → expense splitting (Gap 9) → passenger lane (Gap 5) → join briefing (Gap 4) → share-sheet merge (Gap 2) → pool top-up requests (Gap 7).
